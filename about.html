Our Story

From One Truck to 120+

RescueFleet was founded in 2010 by a former mechanic who understood the frustration and anxiety of being stranded on the road. With a single flatbed tow truck and a commitment to honest, fast service, the company was born.

Over the years, we've grown from that one truck into a fleet of over 120 vehicles, serving 35 cities with more than 250 trained professionals. But our core values haven't changed — we still treat every call like it's our most important one.

Today, we combine cutting-edge technology with hands-on expertise, using GPS tracking and AI routing to ensure the fastest possible response times without sacrificing the personal touch.
